About / Journal

Est. San Francisco

Emordes Studio is an interdisciplinary creative house rooted in fantasy realization with a reconstructed deconstruction aesthetic.

Founded by a designer-stylist and biochemist weaver, we operate at the intersection of science, culture, design, and the collective. We've experimented with biomaterials, developed grand show experiences, and re-imagined ancestral traditions, like our contemporary exploration of the Ilocano kusikus motif.

Driven by devotion to artistry, Emordes Studio offers full-scale services across creative direction, brand development, textile innovation, interior styling, and immersive event and fashion show production. jh@emordes.studio
